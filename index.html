Researched climate and rainfall variability, analyzing impacts across India (West Coast, North India, etc.) from 1901 to 2022. Identified long-term trends, fluctuations, and anomalies using statistical modeling, trend analysis, and spatial mapping. Compared finer vs. coarser resolution data to capture localized variations and analyzed intra-annual variability using CRU, CPC, GPCC, and ERA5 datasets.
