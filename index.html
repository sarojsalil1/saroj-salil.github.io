About Me

Geography, geospatial analysis and climate research

I am a Geography researcher with academic and professional experience in climate variability, rainfall analysis, GIS, remote sensing and geospatial data analysis.

My academic background includes Geography and Applied Geoinformatics, with research experience involving climate datasets, rainfall variability, spatial analysis, terrain analysis and environmental applications of GIS.

My research interests broadly focus on climate variability, extreme weather events, rainfall, hydrology, water resources, geospatial technologies and the relationship between environmental change and society.
