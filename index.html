About Me

I am a Geography researcher with an academic and professional background in GIS, Remote Sensing, climate variability and environmental analysis. My work focuses on understanding geographical and environmental processes through spatial data, Earth observation and quantitative analysis.

My research interests include climate variability, rainfall variability, extreme weather events, hydrology, water resources and environmental applications of geospatial technologies.

I have experience working with GIS and Remote Sensing datasets and software including ArcGIS, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, ERDAS Imagine and Python-based geospatial and statistical analysis.

Currently, my research work also involves spatial analysis and impact assessment related to skill development among tribal youth in Jharkhand.
