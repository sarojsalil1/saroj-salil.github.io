About Me

Geography & Geoinformatics Specialist

Welcome! I am a graduate researcher specializing in hydro-climatology, climate change impact analysis, and geomorphology. My research focuses on understanding long-term climate variability, extreme weather events, and spatial modeling using advanced satellite observation and climate datasets.

Research Specializations

Hydro-climatology, climate trend analysis, coastal hazard assessment, and aeolian dune dynamics.

Technical Expertise

Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib), CDO (Climate Data Operators), ArcGIS, QGIS, and GrADS.

Data & Modeling

Hands-on work with high-resolution climate repositories including ERA5, IMD, CRU, GPCC, and CMIP6 models.
